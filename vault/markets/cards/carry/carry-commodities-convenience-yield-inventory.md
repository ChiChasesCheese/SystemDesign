## Q
Crude oil inventories are unusually low relative to demand. According to the
theory of storage, what should happen to convenience yield, and what does
that predict for the shape of the futures curve — backwardation or contango?

## A
**Convenience yield should rise, and the curve should move toward
backwardation.** Convenience yield is the benefit of physically holding
inventory right now — insurance against a stockout, flexibility to meet an
unexpected order. That benefit is highest exactly when there isn't much
spare supply sitting around: with inventories scarce, the marginal barrel in
hand today is precious (a refiner or consumer badly wants it to avoid
running short), so the market prices a high convenience yield onto physical
holding.

Under the theory-of-storage identity (futures price = spot + financing +
storage − convenience yield), a convenience yield large enough to exceed
financing and storage costs flips the sign of the carry term: the futures
price sits below spot, and near-dated contracts trade above far-dated ones —
backwardation. The opposite case — ample, well-supplied inventories — makes
convenience yield small, so the identity reflects mostly the positive cost
of carry (you're paying to store surplus you don't urgently need), and the
curve sits in contango instead. Inventory tightness is therefore the
economic variable that explains why the same commodity's curve shape
changes over time even when nothing about long-run supply/demand
fundamentals has changed.

## Q zh
原油库存相对需求异常偏低。按照存储理论,便利收益(convenience yield)应该
会发生什么变化?这对期货曲线的形状——backwardation 还是 contango——预示着
什么?

## A zh
**便利收益应该上升,曲线应该向 backwardation 方向移动。** 便利收益是此刻
实物持有库存所带来的好处——对断供的保险、应对突发订单的灵活性。这种好处在
市面上没有多少富余供给的时候恰恰最高:库存紧张时,手上今天多一桶油弥足珍
贵(炼厂或消费者非常需要它来避免供应短缺),所以市场会给实物持有定出一份
很高的便利收益。

在存储理论的恒等式下(期货价格 = 现货 + 融资成本 + 存储成本 − 便利收益),
一份大到超过融资和存储成本的便利收益,会把 carry 这一项的符号反转:期货价
格低于现货,近月合约的价格高于远月合约——即 backwardation。相反的情况——
库存充裕、供给宽松——会让便利收益很小,于是这个恒等式主要反映的是正的持有
成本(你在为存储自己并不急需的过剩库存而付钱),曲线就会呈现 contango。因
此,库存的松紧程度正是解释同一种商品曲线形状会随时间变化的经济变量,即便
长期供需基本面并没有发生任何改变。
