Total return on a carry position splits into {{c1::carry}} plus the spot
price change; carry itself further decomposes into two pieces. The first is
{{c2::yield}} — direct income the position pays while held, such as a bond
coupon, an equity dividend, or an interest-rate differential in an FX
position. The second is {{c3::roll (roll-down / roll yield)}} — the price
change that comes purely from time passing while the position slides along
an *unchanged-shape* curve toward a different point on it: a bond held for a
year on a positively sloped yield curve "rolls down" to a shorter maturity
and richens even if rates never move, and a futures position rolling from an
expiring contract into the next one gains or loses depending on whether the
next contract is priced cheaper or more expensive. A position can have
attractive yield and unattractive roll (or vice versa) — the two pieces are
computed and can move independently, which is why "carry" is never fully
described by yield alone.

## zh
一个 carry 头寸的总收益可以拆成 {{c1::carry}} 加上现货价格变动;carry 本身
又可以进一步拆成两部分。第一部分是 {{c2::yield(票息/收益率)}}——持有期
间头寸直接支付的收入,比如债券票息、股票股息,或者 FX 头寸里的利率差。第
二部分是 {{c3::roll(roll-down/展期收益)}}——纯粹因为时间流逝、头寸沿着一
条**形状不变**的曲线滑向另一个点而产生的价格变化:在一条向上倾斜的收益率
曲线上持有一年,一只债券会"滚动下沉(roll down)"到更短的期限、即使利率
从未变动价格也会走高;而一笔期货头寸从到期合约展期到下一份合约,盈亏取决
于下一份合约的定价是更便宜还是更贵。一个头寸可以 yield 很吸引人但 roll 不
吸引人(反之亦然)——这两部分是分别计算、可以独立变动的,这也是为什么
"carry"从来不能只用 yield 一个数字完整描述。
