## Q
The theory of storage explains commodity curve shape through inventory and
convenience yield. Keynes's theory of "normal backwardation" gives a
different, economic explanation for the same phenomenon, centered on who is
on the other side of a long futures position and why they keep paying.
State that explanation.

## A
**Commodity producers — farmers, oil producers, miners — are natural longs
of the physical good, since their livelihood already depends on its future
price.** To protect against price declines, they want to hedge by selling
futures now, locking in a price for output they haven't yet produced or
sold. But someone has to take the other side and go long those futures,
absorbing the price risk the producer wants to shed.

**To induce speculators to accept that risk, the futures price has to be set
below the market's true expected future spot price** — if the futures price
simply equaled the expected future spot price, a risk-averse speculator
would have no compensation for bearing uncertainty and no reason to take the
long side. By pricing futures below expected future spot ("normal
backwardation"), the long side earns a risk premium on average as the price
tends to rise toward the eventual spot price over the contract's life. This
is the answer to "who pays and why": producers pay for price certainty by
accepting a futures price systematically below their honest expectation of
future spot, and the compensation flows to whoever is willing to hold the
long futures position and absorb the hedging pressure they're offloading.

## Q zh
存储理论通过库存和便利收益来解释商品曲线的形状。凯恩斯的"正常
backwardation(normal backwardation)"理论对同一现象给出了另一种、经济学
意义上的解释,核心在于谁是多头期货头寸的对手盘、以及他们为什么会一直付
钱。请说明这个解释。

## A zh
**商品生产者——农民、石油生产商、矿业公司——天然是实物商品的多头,因为他们
的生计本就依赖于这种商品未来的价格。** 为了防范价格下跌,他们想通过现在卖
出期货来对冲,为尚未生产或出售的产品锁定一个价格。但必须有人愿意站到对面
做多这些期货,承接生产者想甩掉的价格风险。

**为了让投机者愿意接受这个风险,期货价格必须被设定在低于市场对未来现货价
格真实预期的水平。** 如果期货价格恰好等于对未来现货价格的预期,一个风险厌
恶的投机者就得不到任何承担不确定性的补偿,也就没有理由做多。通过把期货价
格定在低于预期未来现货价格的位置("正常 backwardation"),多头一方能够
在合约存续期内,随着价格趋向逐渐上涨、逼近最终的现货价格,平均赚到一份风
险溢价。这就是对"谁付钱、为什么付钱"的回答:生产者通过接受一个系统性低于
自己诚实预期的期货价格来换取价格确定性,而补偿则流向那些愿意持有多头期货
头寸、承接他们想卸掉的套保压力(hedging pressure)的人。
