## Q
Credit carry — holding high-yield bonds to earn the spread over Treasuries —
shares FX carry's basic shape: steady income most of the time, sharp losses
when spreads blow out. Explain why credit and FX carry drawdowns tend to be
sharper and faster than the strategy's calm-period volatility would suggest,
bringing leverage into the explanation rather than just the price move
itself.

## A
**Because carry's attractively smooth, high-Sharpe track record is exactly
what attracts leverage, and leverage is what turns a price move into a
forced-deleveraging spiral.** A strategy that looks like it earns a steady
spread with low volatility invites macro funds and levered vehicles to size
up into it — the position that "should" be modest gets scaled up because the
risk-adjusted return looks so good in normal times.

When a shock hits — a credit-cycle turn, a liquidity crunch, a risk-off
shock in FX — leveraged carry positions don't just experience the initial
price move, they hit margin calls and internal risk limits at roughly the
same time other leveraged holders of the same trade do. Forced selling (or
forced short-covering, in FX) to meet those calls pushes the price further
in the adverse direction, which triggers more margin calls and more forced
unwinding — this is the funding-liquidity/market-liquidity spiral described
by Brunnermeier and Pedersen: a shortage of funding forces asset sales,
which depresses the asset's market liquidity and price, which triggers more
funding shortfalls. The initial shock might have been modest on its own; the
crowded leverage behind carry is what converts it into a drawdown far larger
and faster than the strategy's calm-period volatility would have predicted.

## Q zh
信用 carry——持有高收益债赚取相对国债的利差——和 FX carry 的基本形状是一样
的:大多数时候稳定收租,利差爆炸时出现剧烈亏损。请解释为什么信用和 FX
carry 的回撤往往比策略平静期的波动率所暗示的更剧烈、更迅速,并且要把杠杆
而不只是价格变动本身纳入解释。

## A zh
**因为 carry 那种吸引人的、平滑的高夏普业绩记录,恰恰正是吸引杠杆的原因,
而杠杆正是把一次价格变动变成一场强制去杠杆螺旋的原因。** 一个看起来能以低
波动率稳定赚取利差的策略,会吸引宏观基金和加杠杆的载体加大在其中的仓位规
模——一个"本应"是适度规模的头寸,会因为正常时期风险调整后收益看起来太好而
被放大。

当冲击来袭时——一次信用周期转折、一次流动性紧缩、一次 FX 中的风险规避冲
击——加了杠杆的 carry 头寸不只是承受最初的价格变动,它们还会在大致相同的
时间遭遇追加保证金和内部风险限额的触发,和持有同一笔交易的其他加杠杆主体
一样。为满足这些追加保证金而进行的强制卖出(在 FX 里则是强制回补空头)会
把价格进一步推向不利方向,从而触发更多的追加保证金和更多的强制平仓——这就
是 Brunnermeier 和 Pedersen 描述的融资流动性/市场流动性螺旋:融资短缺迫使
资产被抛售,这压低了资产的市场流动性和价格,进而触发更多的融资短缺。最初
的冲击本身也许并不算大;正是 carry 背后拥挤的杠杆,把它转化成一场比策略平
静期波动率所能预测的更大、更快的回撤。
