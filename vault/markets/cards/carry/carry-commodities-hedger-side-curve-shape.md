## Q
Keynesian hedging pressure is often summarized as "commodity futures curves
are normally backwardated because producers hedge." Natural gas ahead of
winter, however, frequently trades in contango, with utilities and other
consumers aggressively buying forward to lock in supply. Does this
contradict the hedging-pressure theory, or does it reveal something the
"producers always hedge" summary leaves out?

## A
**It reveals what the summary leaves out: hedging pressure theory is about
whichever side of the market has the more urgent, price-inelastic need to
hedge — it does not say that side is always the producer.** The mechanism is
symmetric. If producers are the desperate hedgers, they sell futures to
shed price risk, and the futures price gets bid down below expected future
spot to compensate the long speculators who absorb that risk —
backwardation.

If instead it's consumers who urgently need price certainty — an airline
locking in jet fuel, a utility locking in natural gas ahead of a winter
demand spike where a shortage would be operationally catastrophic — they are
the ones buying futures aggressively to hedge, and it's now the *short*
futures speculator who needs to be compensated for taking the other side of
that buying pressure. That compensation comes from the futures price being
bid *above* expected future spot — contango.

The general rule, then, is not "commodities are backwardated" but "a
commodity's structural curve bias reflects which side of the market —
producers or consumers — has the more urgent, inelastic hedging demand, and
compensates whichever side of the futures market is left absorbing that
pressure." Different commodities, and even the same commodity in different
seasons, can sit on either side of that split.

## Q zh
凯恩斯式套保压力(hedging pressure)理论常被概括为"商品期货曲线通常呈
backwardation,因为生产者要对冲"。但天然气在冬季来临前却经常呈现
contango,公用事业公司等消费者大举买入远期以锁定供应。这是否与套保压力理
论矛盾?还是说它揭示了"生产者总是对冲"这个概括所遗漏的东西?

## A zh
**它揭示的是这个概括所遗漏的东西:套保压力理论说的是市场上哪一方有更迫
切、对价格更不敏感的对冲需求,并不是说这一方永远是生产者。** 这个机制是对
称的。如果生产者是那个急于对冲的一方,他们卖出期货以甩掉价格风险,期货价
格就会被压低到低于预期未来现货价格,以此补偿承接这份风险的多头投机者——形
成 backwardation。

反过来,如果是消费者迫切需要价格确定性——比如航空公司锁定航空燃油,或者公
用事业公司在冬季需求高峰前锁定天然气,一旦供应短缺在运营上将是灾难性
的——那么就是他们在积极买入期货以对冲,这时需要被补偿的就变成了**空头**期
货投机者,因为是他们在承接这份买盘压力的对面。这份补偿的来源就是期货价格
被抬高到**超过**预期未来现货价格——形成 contango。

所以一般规律并不是"商品都是 backwardation",而是"一种商品结构性曲线偏
向,反映的是市场上哪一方——生产者还是消费者——有更迫切、更缺乏弹性的对冲需
求,并补偿留在期货市场对面、承接这份压力的那一方"。不同商品之间,甚至同一
种商品在不同季节,都可能落在这个分界的任意一侧。
