The theory of storage prices a commodity future as {{c1::F = S × (1 +
financing cost + storage cost − convenience yield)}} — spot price, plus the
cost of physically carrying the commodity forward to delivery (financing the
purchase plus storage and insurance), minus a {{c2::convenience yield}}.
Convenience yield is the non-monetary benefit of holding the physical
commodity itself rather than a paper claim on it — the ability to keep a
factory running, avoid a costly stockout, or respond to an unexpected demand
spike — a benefit that {{c3::a futures contract cannot provide}}, since it
only delivers exposure to the price, not the physical barrel or bushel on
hand today.

## zh
存储理论(theory of storage)把商品期货价格定为 {{c1::F = S × (1 + 融资成
本 + 存储成本 − 便利收益)}}——现货价格,加上把这个商品实物持有到交割日所需
的持有成本(购买所需的融资成本,加上仓储和保险费),再减去一份
{{c2::便利收益(convenience yield)}}。便利收益是持有实物商品本身、而不是
一份纸面价格敞口所带来的非货币收益——比如能让工厂持续运转、避免代价高昂的
断供、或者应对突发的需求飙升——这是一种{{c3::期货合约无法提供}}的收益,因
为期货合约只给你价格敞口,而不给你今天手上实实在在的一桶油或一蒲式耳粮
食。
