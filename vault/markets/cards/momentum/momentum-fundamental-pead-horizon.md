## Q
A company reports earnings that beat expectations by a wide margin (a high
standardized unexpected earnings, or SUE). Under semi-strong-form market
efficiency, the stock should reprice essentially immediately once the number
is public. What does post-earnings-announcement drift (PEAD) say actually
happens, over what horizon, and why is this considered one of the most
direct violations of efficiency in the anomalies literature?

## A
**The stock keeps drifting in the direction of the surprise for roughly
60 trading days — about one quarter — after the announcement**, rather than
jumping immediately to its new fair value. High-SUE stocks continue earning
positive abnormal returns for weeks to months after the beat; low-SUE
(earnings miss) stocks continue underperforming the same way. The size of
the subsequent drift is predictable from the size of the initial surprise —
bigger surprises produce more drift, not just a bigger one-day jump.

This is considered one of the most direct efficiency violations (dating to
Ball and Brown 1968, formalized with the SUE measure by Bernard and Thomas
1989) precisely because the triggering information — the earnings number —
is fully public the moment it's announced. There is no private-information
story available: everyone has the same number at the same time, yet the
price keeps moving predictably in its direction for months, which is exactly
what semi-strong efficiency says should not happen to publicly available
information.

## Q zh
一家公司公布的财报大幅超出预期(即标准化未预期盈余 SUE 很高)。按照半强式
市场有效性,一旦这个数字公开,股价应该几乎立刻重新定价到位。盈余公告后漂
移(PEAD)实际上说会发生什么,在多长的时间跨度内发生,以及为什么这在异象
文献里被认为是对有效性最直接的违背之一?

## A zh
**股价会在公告后沿着惊喜的方向继续漂移大约 60 个交易日——约一个季度**,而
不是立刻跳到新的公允价值。SUE 高的股票在超预期之后的几周到几个月里会持续
获得正的超额收益;SUE 低(不及预期)的股票会以同样的方式持续跑输。后续漂
移的幅度可以由最初惊喜的幅度预测出来——更大的惊喜带来更多的漂移,而不只是
更大的单日跳空。

这被认为是最直接的有效性违背之一(最早见于 Ball and Brown 1968,由
Bernard and Thomas 1989 用 SUE 指标正式化),正是因为触发信息——盈余数
字——在公告的那一刻就已经完全公开了。这里没有私有信息的解释空间:所有人同
一时间拿到同一个数字,但价格却在接下来的几个月里持续沿着这个方向可预测地
移动,这正是半强式有效市场理论认为不应该发生在公开信息上的事情。
