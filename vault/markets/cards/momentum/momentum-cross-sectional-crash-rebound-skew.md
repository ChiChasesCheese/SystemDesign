## Q
The market has just bottomed after a sharp decline and begins a violent
rebound off the low — think March 2009. What happens to a standard
long-winners/short-losers momentum portfolio in exactly this moment, and why
does this produce the strategy's characteristic negative skew?

## A
**The short leg gets destroyed.** Going into the bottom, the "losers" bucket
is disproportionately full of high-beta, high-volatility stocks — the names
that fell the hardest in the preceding crash are, mechanically, the ones with
the most market exposure and the most operating/financial leverage. When the
market snaps back sharply, those same high-beta losers rebound even harder
than the market, because beta amplifies the move in both directions. Momentum
is short exactly that basket, so the short leg loses enormously in a short
window, while the long leg (lower-beta past winners) only partially offsets
it on the way up.

This is a **momentum crash** (Daniel–Moskowitz): it doesn't happen randomly
or often, but when it happens it is large and concentrated in a few months
right after a market bottom, which is precisely what generates momentum's
strongly negative skew — long stretches of modest, steady gains punctuated by
rare, severe drawdowns tied to a specific and identifiable regime (a violent
rebound off a beaten-down market), not to momentum failing gradually.

## Q zh
市场刚刚触底,并在低点后开始猛烈反弹——就像 2009 年 3 月那样。在这个时点,
一个标准的"买赢家、空输家"动量组合会发生什么?为什么这会产生动量策略特有
的负偏(negative skew)?

## A zh
**空头腿被打爆。** 在触底之前,"输家"一档里大量是高 beta、高波动的股票——
前面那波暴跌中跌得最惨的股票,机械地看,恰恰是市场暴露最大、经营/财务杠杆
最高的那批。当市场猛烈反弹时,这些高 beta 的输家股票反弹幅度会**超过**大盘
本身,因为 beta 在两个方向上都会放大波动。动量组合正好做空这一篮子股票,
所以在很短的窗口内空头腿会巨亏,而多头腿(beta 较低的前期赢家)在上涨中只
能部分对冲这个损失。

这就是**动量崩溃(momentum crash,Daniel–Moskowitz)**:它不是随机、频繁发
生的,但一旦发生就集中在市场触底后的几个月内、幅度巨大——这正是动量收益呈
现强烈负偏的原因:长期温和稳定的小赚,被少数几次和特定、可辨识的状态(触底
后暴力反弹)绑定的严重回撤打断,而不是动量缓慢失效。
