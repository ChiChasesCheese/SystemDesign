## Q
Analyst earnings-estimate revisions predict future stock returns in a way
that looks a lot like PEAD — stocks with recent upward EPS revisions keep
outperforming — but the "surprise" here is a forecast, not a realized
number. Explain what's actually being underreacted to, and why this counts
as fundamental momentum's slower-moving cousin rather than a duplicate of
PEAD.

## A
**The information being slowly digested is the analyst's own updated
forecast, not the company's realized earnings.** When an analyst raises an
EPS estimate — because of a strong preliminary indicator, a channel check, a
sector read-through — that revision itself carries information about the
company's prospects that the market doesn't fully price in immediately.
Investors, and other analysts, adjust their own views toward the revision
gradually rather than instantly, so the stock keeps drifting in the
direction of the revision for a stretch after it's published (Chan,
Jegadeesh, and Lakonishok).

It is fundamental momentum's slower cousin rather than a duplicate of PEAD
because the trigger and the cadence differ: PEAD is anchored to a discrete,
quarterly, fully public earnings print with a defined ~60-day drift window;
analyst revisions arrive continuously and asynchronously throughout the
quarter as new information trickles into forecasts, so the resulting drift
is spread out and overlapping rather than clustered around a single
calendar event. Both share the same underlying story — gradual diffusion of
fundamental information into price rather than instant repricing — but they
are two distinct, only partially overlapping ways that story shows up in the
data.

## Q zh
分析师盈利预测修正能预测未来股票收益,方式看起来很像 PEAD——近期被上调
EPS 预测的股票会持续跑赢——但这里的"惊喜"是一个预测,而不是一个已实现的
数字。请说明这里市场真正反应不足的对象是什么,以及为什么这算是基本面动量
更慢一拍的表亲,而不是 PEAD 的重复。

## A zh
**被市场慢慢消化的信息,是分析师自己更新后的预测,而不是公司已实现的盈
利。** 当一位分析师上调 EPS 预测时——因为看到了强劲的先行指标、渠道调研、
或行业层面的传导信号——这次修正本身就携带了关于公司前景的信息,市场并不
会立刻把它完全定价进去。投资者以及其他分析师会逐步而非立刻地把自己的看法
向这次修正靠拢,所以股价会在修正发布后的一段时间里继续朝修正的方向漂移
(Chan、Jegadeesh 与 Lakonishok)。

它之所以是基本面动量更慢一拍的表亲,而不是 PEAD 的重复,是因为触发事件和
节奏不同:PEAD 锚定在一个离散的、按季度、完全公开的盈利公告上,有一个明确
的约 60 个交易日的漂移窗口;而分析师修正在整个季度里持续、异步地发生,随
着新信息一点点渗入预测,由此产生的漂移是分散、相互叠加的,而不是集中在某
一个日历事件周围。两者背后是同一个故事——基本面信息缓慢扩散进价格,而不
是瞬间重新定价——但它们是这个故事在数据里表现出来的两种不同、只部分重叠
的方式。
