## Q
PEAD has been documented since 1968 and is one of the most heavily studied
anomalies in finance, yet it hasn't been fully arbitraged away. Give the two
behavioral/structural mechanisms usually cited for why the underreaction
persists, and explain why the size of the anomaly itself is part of the
reason arbitrage doesn't close it.

## A
**Limited attention:** investors have finite time and processing capacity
and cannot fully absorb and act on every earnings release, every day, across
thousands of stocks — Hong and Stein's gradual-information-diffusion
framework treats this as a real constraint, not irrationality per se.
Information genuinely takes time to reach and be processed by all the market
participants who would need to trade on it for the price to jump instantly.

**Slow institutional diffusion:** even investors who do notice the surprise
often can't act on it all at once — mandates, transaction-cost budgets, and
gradual position-building mean institutional flow into the stock happens
over weeks, not the same afternoon, which mechanically stretches the price
adjustment out over time rather than compressing it into the announcement
day.

**Why the anomaly's own size limits arbitrage:** the drift on any single
stock is modest — a few percent over a couple of months — and noisy at the
individual-name level, so the expected profit per unit of capital and risk
committed is small relative to trading costs, market-impact costs, and the
opportunity cost of research/data infrastructure needed to trade it broadly
and precisely (accurate SUE requires costly analyst-consensus data, not
free historical earnings). Because the edge is thin per trade, only a
finite amount of capital finds it worth deploying against, which is exactly
what limits to arbitrage predicts keeps a small, genuine anomaly alive
rather than instantly priced away.

## Q zh
PEAD 从 1968 年就被记录下来,是金融学里被研究得最透彻的异象之一,但它并没
有被完全套利消除。请给出通常用来解释这种反应不足为何能持续存在的两个行为
/结构性机制,并说明为什么异象本身的规模也是套利无法把它抹平的原因之一。

## A zh
**注意力有限(limited attention):** 投资者的时间和处理能力是有限的,不可
能对每天、成千上万只股票的每一份财报都完全消化并据此交易——Hong 和 Stein
的渐进信息扩散框架把这看作一个真实的约束,而不完全是非理性。信息确实需要
时间才能传达到、并被所有需要据此交易的市场参与者处理完,价格才可能瞬间跳
位。

**机构调仓缓慢:** 即便有投资者注意到了这个惊喜,往往也无法一次性全部据此
交易——授权范围、交易成本预算、以及分批建仓的做法,意味着机构资金流入这只
股票需要几周时间,而不是当天下午,这机械地把价格调整拉长到一段时间里,而
不是压缩在公告当天完成。

**为什么异象本身的规模限制了套利:** 单只股票上的漂移幅度不大——两个月里几
个百分点——而且在个股层面很嘈杂,所以相对于交易成本、市场冲击成本、以及要
广泛精确地交易这个信号所需的研究/数据基础设施的机会成本(准确的 SUE 需要
昂贵的分析师一致预期数据,而不是免费的历史盈利数据)而言,每单位资金和风
险所能获得的预期利润很小。因为每笔交易的 edge 很薄,只有有限的资金会觉得
值得投入进去套利,这正是"套利限制(limits to arbitrage)"理论所预测
的——一个小而真实的异象因此得以存活,而不是被瞬间定价消除。
