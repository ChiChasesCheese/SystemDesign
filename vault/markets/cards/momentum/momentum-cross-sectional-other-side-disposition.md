## Q
Cross-sectional momentum needs a counterparty who is systematically willing
to sell winners too cheap and hold losers too long. Name the specific
behavioral mechanism that supplies this counterparty, and explain how it
mechanically produces the drift momentum trades on.

## A
**The disposition effect.** Investors tend to sell winning positions too
early, to lock in a gain and feel good about it, while holding losing
positions too long, hoping to avoid realizing a loss. When good news arrives
about a stock, disposition-prone holders sell into the rally to bank the
profit — this selling pressure absorbs part of the price reaction and keeps
the stock from jumping immediately to its new fair value. The rest of the
adjustment then has to happen gradually, over the following weeks and
months, as new buyers arrive and the disposition-driven sellers are
exhausted — that gradual catch-up is exactly the drift a momentum strategy
harvests.

The same mechanism works in reverse on bad news: disposition-prone holders
of a losing stock refuse to sell and realize the loss, which slows the price
from falling all the way to fair value immediately, so it keeps drifting down
for a while after the news instead.

A second, related supplier is slow-moving capital — analysts revise
forecasts gradually rather than instantly, and institutions rebalance in
stages — which reinforces the same underreaction-then-drift pattern rather
than a one-time repricing.

## Q zh
横截面动量需要一个愿意系统性地"赢家卖得太早、输家拿得太久"的对手盘。指出
具体供给这个对手盘的行为机制是什么,并说明它是如何机械地产生动量所交易的
那段漂移的。

## A zh
**处置效应(disposition effect)。** 投资者倾向于过早卖出盈利头寸(为了锁定
收益、获得心理满足),同时把亏损头寸拿得太久(不愿意兑现亏损)。当一只股票
出现好消息时,受处置效应影响的持有者会趁着上涨卖出以落袋为安——这部分卖压
吸收了一部分价格反应,让股价无法立刻跳到新的公允价值。剩下的调整就只能在
接下来的几周到几个月里慢慢发生,随着新买家进场、受处置效应驱动的卖家被消
耗殆尽——这段缓慢的补涨/补跌正是动量策略赚的那段漂移。

同样的机制在坏消息上反向起作用:持有亏损股票的、受处置效应影响的人拒绝卖出
兑现亏损,这拖慢了股价立即跌到公允价值的速度,于是股价在消息公布后会继续
慢慢下跌一段时间。

第二个相关的供给方是慢钱(slow-moving capital)——分析师逐步而非立即修正
预测,机构分批而非一次性调仓——这强化的是同一种"先反应不足、后慢慢漂移"
的模式,而不是一次性的重新定价。
