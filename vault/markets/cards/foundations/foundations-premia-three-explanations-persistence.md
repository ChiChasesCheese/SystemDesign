## Q
Name the three broad explanations economists give for why an observed return premium exists, and explain why only one of them predicts the premium will keep paying after the strategy has been published in an academic paper and become well known.

## A
- **Compensation for risk.** The premium is fair, rational payment for bearing a risk other investors want to avoid — most tellingly, one whose losses cluster in bad economic states ("bad returns in bad times," e.g. a recession or a liquidity crisis). Publicizing this kind of premium does not remove the underlying risk, so smart money piling in cannot arbitrage it away — at most it gets priced slightly more efficiently, not eliminated.
- **Behavioural.** The premium exists because some investors are making a systematic, repeated cognitive mistake (extrapolating recent price moves, anchoring on a reference price). Once documented and widely understood, sophisticated capital trades against the mistake and the premium shrinks or disappears — McLean and Pontiff (2016) found published cross-sectional return anomalies decayed by roughly 58% out of sample after publication, consistent with this channel.
- **Structural / institutional.** Some participants are forced to trade for reasons unrelated to expected return — index funds rebalancing on a fixed schedule, insurers matching regulatory duration targets, pension funds constrained to investment grade. This can persist indefinitely because the rule doesn't go away, but it is usually capacity-limited: the profit available is bounded by the size of the forced flow.

Only the first — genuine compensation for risk — is expected to keep paying once everyone knows about it, because knowledge of a risk premium doesn't neutralize the risk itself; the other two either get arbitraged down (behavioural) or are capped by how much forced flow exists (structural).

## Q zh
说出经济学家用来解释"为什么会存在一个观察到的收益溢价"的三大类原因，并说明为什么只有其中一类能预测：即使这个策略已经被写成学术论文发表、变得人尽皆知，这个溢价依然会继续支付。

## A zh
- **风险补偿（compensation for risk）。** 这份溢价是对承担其他投资者不愿承担的风险的合理、理性补偿——最具代表性的特征是：亏损集中在经济糟糕的时期（"坏时候赚坏收益"，bad returns in bad times，比如衰退或流动性危机）。把这类溢价公开出来，并不会消除背后那个风险本身，所以聪明钱涌入也无法把它套利掉——最多让定价更有效一点，而不会让它消失。
- **行为偏差（behavioural）。** 这份溢价的存在，是因为一部分投资者在系统性地、反复地犯同一类认知错误（比如把近期的价格走势线性外推、锚定在某个参考价格上）。一旦这个错误被记录、被广泛理解，聪明资金就会站到这个错误的对面去交易，溢价随之收窄乃至消失——McLean 和 Pontiff（2016）发现，学术界发表过的横截面收益异象在发表之后的样本外，平均衰减了约 58%，与这个机制吻合。
- **结构性 / 制度性（structural）。** 有些参与者交易并不是为了追求预期收益，而是被规则逼着交易——指数基金按固定时间表再平衡、保险公司为匹配监管久期要求而配置、养老金被限定只能持有投资级债券。这类溢价可以无限期存在，因为那条规则不会消失；但它通常受容量限制：能赚到的利润被这股被迫交易的资金流规模所限定。

只有第一类——真正的风险补偿——才能预期在人尽皆知之后继续支付，因为知道一个风险溢价的存在，并不会中和掉那个风险本身；另外两类要么会被套利磨薄（行为偏差），要么受限于被迫交易的资金规模上限（结构性）。
