## Q
Mehra and Prescott (1985) identified the "equity premium puzzle." What exactly is puzzling about the historical equity risk premium, and what does resolving it require economists to give up or add to the standard model?

## A
**Under the standard consumption-based asset pricing model, an asset's risk premium should scale with investors' risk aversion times the covariance between its returns and consumption growth.** Equity returns are not that volatile relative to consumption, and consumption itself is fairly smooth from year to year, so the model predicts only a small equity premium unless investors are assumed to be extremely risk-averse. Mehra and Prescott calculated that matching the observed roughly 6% historical premium over bills requires a coefficient of relative risk aversion above about 30-40, when values in the low single digits (1-2) are what economists consider psychologically plausible based on other evidence, such as how people behave in small-stakes gambles.

The puzzle forces a choice: either investors are far more risk-averse than assumed elsewhere in economics, or the standard model is missing something. Proposed fixes include rare, severe "disaster" states not well represented in the twentieth-century US sample (Rietz, Barro), limited stock market participation that concentrates equity risk on a smaller, more risk-averse group of actual stockholders rather than all consumers, and behavioural loss aversion (Benartzi-Thaler) making investors who evaluate their returns too frequently demand extra compensation for volatility they would tolerate if they checked their portfolio less often. No single resolution is fully settled, which is why the "puzzle" label has stuck for 40 years.

## Q zh
Mehra 和 Prescott（1985）提出了"股权溢价之谜（equity premium puzzle）"。历史股权风险溢价到底哪里"谜"了？要解开这个谜，经济学家的标准模型需要放弃或补上什么？

## A zh
**按标准的消费基资产定价模型（consumption-based asset pricing model），一项资产的风险溢价应当等于投资者的风险厌恶程度乘以其收益率与消费增长的协方差。** 但股票收益相对消费的波动其实没有那么大，而消费本身逐年变化也相当平稳，所以除非假设投资者极度厌恶风险，否则该模型只能算出很小的股权溢价。Mehra 和 Prescott 计算出，要匹配历史上相对国库券大约 6% 的观测溢价，所需要的相对风险厌恶系数要超过大约 30-40；而经济学家根据其他证据（比如人们在小额赌局中的行为）认为，心理上合理的取值只在个位数低端（1-2）。

这个谜迫使人做出选择：要么投资者实际的风险厌恶程度，远高于经济学在其他场景里所假设的水平；要么标准模型漏掉了什么。已有的修补方案包括：二十世纪美国样本里没能充分体现的、罕见而剧烈的"灾难"状态（Rietz、Barro）；有限的股市参与度，使股票风险实际上集中在一小群风险厌恶程度更高的真正持股者身上，而不是分摊给全体消费者；以及行为金融中的损失厌恶（Benartzi-Thaler）——那些过于频繁地检视自己收益的投资者，会为本可以在较少查看组合时容忍的波动，要求额外的补偿。这些方案没有哪一个被完全公认解决了问题，这也是为什么这个"谜"的说法能流传 40 年之久。
