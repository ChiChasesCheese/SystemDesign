## Q
A trader claims to earn consistent, statistically significant excess returns using only a rule based on past price and volume data — no fundamentals, no news, no private information. Assuming the claim is true and not a statistical artifact, which specific form of the Efficient Market Hypothesis does this directly contradict, and why doesn't that same evidence automatically prove market inefficiency (tie this back to the joint hypothesis problem)?

## A
**This directly contradicts weak-form efficiency**, which specifically claims that current prices already incorporate all information contained in the history of prices and trading volume, so no rule built only from that history should produce risk-adjusted excess returns. Because weak-form efficiency is the weakest of Fama's three forms and is logically implied by both semi-strong- and strong-form efficiency, a genuine weak-form violation would falsify all three simultaneously.

However, "risk-adjusted" is doing a lot of work in that sentence, and this is exactly where the joint hypothesis problem re-enters: to call the return "excess," the trader (or a researcher evaluating the claim) had to choose a model of required return — if that model omits a real risk factor the price-based rule happens to load on (for instance, a momentum rule that is secretly taking on more crash risk or higher volatility exposure than the benchmark accounts for), the "excess" return could be fair compensation for an uncaptured risk rather than genuine weak-form inefficiency. The price-based pattern is real evidence against weak-form efficiency, but whether it reflects true inefficiency or a mismeasured risk premium can never be settled by the return series alone.

## Q zh
某交易者声称，只靠一条基于历史价格和成交量的规则——不用基本面、不用新闻、不用非公开信息——就能持续赚到统计显著的超额收益。假设这个说法为真、不是统计假象，这直接违反了有效市场假说（EMH）的哪一种具体形式？为什么同样的证据不能自动证明市场无效（请把这一点和联合假设问题联系起来）？

## A zh
**这直接违反了弱式有效（weak-form efficiency）**，它明确主张当前价格已经吸收了价格和成交量历史中包含的全部信息，所以任何仅靠这段历史构造出的规则都不应该能产生风险调整后的超额收益。由于弱式有效是 Fama 三种形式里最弱的一种，而半强式有效和强式有效在逻辑上都蕴含着弱式有效，所以一次真正的弱式有效违反，会同时证伪这三种形式。

不过，这句话里"风险调整后"这个限定词分量很重，而联合假设问题正是在这里重新出现：要把这份收益称为"超额"，这位交易者（或评估这个说法的研究者）必须先选定一个必要收益模型——如果这个模型恰好遗漏了这条基于价格的规则暗中承担的某个真实风险因子（比如，一条动量规则实际上偷偷承担了比基准所计入的更高的崩盘风险或波动率敞口），那么这份"超额"收益完全可能是对某个未被捕捉到的风险的合理补偿，而不是真正的弱式无效。这个基于价格的现象，是反对弱式有效的真实证据，但它究竟反映的是真实的市场无效，还是一个被错误度量的风险溢价，永远无法仅凭收益序列本身来一锤定音。
