Fama's Efficient Market Hypothesis comes in three nested forms, from weakest to strongest claim: {{c1::weak-form efficiency}} — current prices already reflect all information contained in past prices and trading volume, so technical analysis (chart patterns, momentum in the price series itself) cannot generate risk-adjusted excess returns; {{c2::semi-strong-form efficiency}} — prices reflect all publicly available information (financial statements, news, macro data), so fundamental analysis of public data cannot generate excess returns, and prices should adjust to new public information essentially instantly; {{c3::strong-form efficiency}} — prices reflect all information, public and private, so not even a corporate insider trading on material non-public information could systematically beat the market. Each stronger form implies the weaker ones; strong-form efficiency is the one most decisively rejected by evidence (insiders and some professional investors do earn excess returns), while weak- and semi-strong-form efficiency remain far more empirically defensible, especially in the most liquid, widely covered markets.

## zh
Fama 的有效市场假说（Efficient Market Hypothesis, EMH）有三种层层嵌套的形式，从最弱到最强依次是：{{c1::弱式有效（weak-form efficiency）}}——当前价格已经反映了过去价格和交易量中包含的全部信息，所以技术分析（图表形态、价格序列自身的动量）无法产生风险调整后的超额收益；{{c2::半强式有效（semi-strong-form efficiency）}}——价格反映了所有公开可得的信息（财务报表、新闻、宏观数据），所以基于公开数据的基本面分析无法产生超额收益，且价格应当在新的公开信息出现后近乎瞬间调整完毕；{{c3::强式有效（strong-form efficiency）}}——价格反映了包括公开和非公开在内的全部信息，所以即便是掌握重大非公开信息的公司内部人交易，也无法系统性地跑赢市场。每一种更强的形式都蕴含着比它弱的形式；强式有效是被证据最彻底否定的一种（内部人和部分专业投资者确实能赚到超额收益），而弱式有效和半强式有效在实证上仍站得住脚得多，尤其是在流动性最好、被最多人研究覆盖的市场里。
