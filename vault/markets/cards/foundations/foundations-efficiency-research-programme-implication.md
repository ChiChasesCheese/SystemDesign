## Q
Given the Grossman-Stiglitz result that markets cannot be perfectly efficient, what does "the market is highly (though not perfectly) efficient" actually imply for how you should design a quantitative research programme — what should you look for, and what should raise your suspicion?

## A
**It implies that any edge you find must be paid for by a real, identifiable cost or friction, and its size should be roughly proportional to that cost — not free money sitting in the most liquid, most-studied names.** A sound research programme deliberately searches where the Grossman-Stiglitz mechanism predicts residual inefficiency survives: data that is expensive or effortful to acquire and clean (point-in-time fundamentals rather than restated data, alternative data requiring bespoke pipelines), securities that capital is structurally reluctant or unable to reach (small/micro-cap names below institutional capacity thresholds, thinly traded credit), and time horizons or holding costs (illiquidity, leverage constraints, career/mandate risk) that deter competition even when the raw signal is visible.

It should raise immediate suspicion when a strategy shows a large, clean edge on data that is cheap, public, and heavily studied — decades-old daily closing prices on the S&P 500, say — because Grossman-Stiglitz says that is exactly where competition should have already driven the exploitable inefficiency down toward the cost of trading it. The honest research question becomes "who is paying for this edge and why hasn't cheaper competition already removed it," not "did my backtest find a positive Sharpe ratio."

## Q zh
既然 Grossman-Stiglitz 的结论是市场不可能完全有效，那么"市场高度有效（但不是完全有效）"这个事实，对你应该如何设计一个量化研究项目有什么实际含义？你应该去哪里找信号？看到什么情况应该起疑心？

## A zh
**它意味着，你找到的任何 edge 都必须由某种真实、可辨识的成本或摩擦来"埋单"，而且它的大小应当大致与那份成本成比例——而不是流动性最好、被研究得最透的那些标的上摆着的白捡的钱。** 一个靠谱的研究项目会刻意去 Grossman-Stiglitz 机制预测残余无效性会存活下来的地方寻找：获取和清洗成本高、费工夫的数据（point-in-time 基本面数据而非事后修订过的数据、需要定制化处理流程的另类数据）；资本在结构上不愿意或无法触及的证券（低于机构容量门槛的小盘/微盘股、交易稀薄的信用债）；以及即便原始信号肉眼可见，也会因为非流动性、杠杆约束、职业生涯/授权限制（career/mandate risk）等持有成本而吓退竞争的时间尺度。

反过来，如果一个策略在便宜、公开、被研究得极其透彻的数据上（比如标普 500 成分股几十年的日收盘价）显示出巨大、干净的 edge，就应该立刻起疑心——因为按 Grossman-Stiglitz 的逻辑，竞争本该早已把这类地方可利用的无效性压到接近交易成本的水平。诚实的研究问题应当是"这份 edge 是谁在买单，为什么更便宜的竞争者没有把它抢走"，而不是"我的回测有没有跑出一个正的夏普比率"。
