Ilmanen decomposes the expected return of any portfolio into four building blocks, roughly ordered from most mechanical ("beta-like") to most skill-dependent: {{c1::asset class risk premia}} — the return from simply holding equities, bonds, credit and other asset classes long, earned for their broad market risk; the {{c2::illiquidity premium}} — extra return for holding assets (private equity, real estate, thinly traded bonds) that cannot be sold quickly at a fair price; {{c3::style/factor premia}} — returns from systematic long-short tilts such as value, momentum, carry, and defensive/low-risk, captured net of the broad asset-class exposure; and {{c4::alpha}} — skill-based return left over after all three of the above are accounted for, which is zero-sum across investors before costs.

## zh
Ilmanen 把任意组合的预期收益拆成四大来源，大致按"从最机械（最像 beta）到最依赖技能"排序：{{c1::资产类别风险溢价（asset class risk premia）}}——单纯长期持有股票、债券、信用等资产类别所赚到的收益，是对承担这些资产广义市场风险的补偿；{{c2::非流动性溢价（illiquidity premium）}}——持有无法迅速以公允价格卖出的资产（私募股权、房地产、交易稀薄的债券）所获得的额外收益；{{c3::风格/因子溢价（style/factor premia）}}——价值、动量、carry、防御型/低风险等系统性多空敞口所赚到的收益，是扣除了大类资产敞口之后剩下的部分；以及 {{c4::alpha}}——在扣除以上三项之后剩下的、依赖技能的那部分收益，在扣除费用前，它在所有投资者之间是零和的。
