## Q
Private equity and real estate funds routinely lock up investor capital for 7-10 years, and their reported returns are often cited as beating public equities. What does the illiquidity premium these vehicles claim to earn actually compensate an investor for, and why is comparing their headline return directly to a liquid public benchmark misleading?

## A
**The illiquidity premium compensates an investor for being unable to sell an asset quickly at a fair price when circumstances demand it** — for bearing the risk that they will need cash, or want to rebalance, precisely when the asset is hardest to exit (a crisis, when buyers vanish and bid-ask spreads blow out), plus the direct search and transaction costs of eventually finding a buyer. It is priced two ways: at purchase, an illiquid asset trades at a discount relative to an economically similar liquid one because short-horizon investors demand extra compensation to hold it (the Amihud-Mendelson clientele effect); over the holding period, it is expected to earn a higher return than a liquid equivalent precisely because that discount unwinds toward fair value only slowly, and because holders cannot opportunistically time their exit.

Comparing headline private-market returns to public benchmarks is misleading because private valuations are appraisal-based and updated infrequently (quarterly, by the manager), which mechanically smooths reported volatility and understates true risk — a private asset isn't actually less risky than a similar public one, it just isn't marked to market often enough to show it. Some of the apparent "premium" is real compensation for illiquidity risk; some of it is an artifact of stale, smoothed pricing that flatters both the return and the risk-adjusted (Sharpe-style) comparison.

## Q zh
私募股权（PE）和房地产基金通常会把投资者的资金锁定 7-10 年，其披露的收益也常被拿来宣称跑赢了公开市场股票。这类工具号称赚到的非流动性溢价（illiquidity premium），实际上补偿的是什么？为什么直接拿它们的表面收益跟流动性好的公开市场基准比较是有误导性的？

## A zh
**非流动性溢价补偿的是投资者在情况需要时，无法迅速以公允价格卖出资产这一事实**——补偿的是这样一种风险：恰好在最难退出的时候（比如危机中，买家消失、买卖价差急剧扩大）需要用钱或想要再平衡；再加上最终找到买家所需的直接搜寻和交易成本。它体现在两个层面：在买入时，一项非流动性资产相对经济性质相似的流动性资产会有折价，因为短期限投资者要求额外补偿才愿意持有它（Amihud-Mendelson 的客户效应）；在持有期内，它被预期赚取比流动性等价物更高的收益，恰恰是因为这个折价只会缓慢地向公允价值收敛，而且持有人无法机会主义地择时退出。

把私募市场的表面收益直接拿去跟公开市场基准比较是有误导性的，因为私募资产的估值基于评估（通常由管理人按季度更新），这在机制上把披露的波动率人为平滑了，从而低估了真实风险——一项私募资产并不是真的比类似的公开市场资产风险更低，只是没有被足够频繁地按市值计价（mark to market），所以没有把风险显现出来。表面上的"溢价"，一部分是对非流动性风险的真实补偿；另一部分则是陈旧、平滑定价带来的假象，它同时美化了收益和风险调整后（类夏普比率）的比较结果。
