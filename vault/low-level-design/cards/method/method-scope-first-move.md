## Q
The prompt is one line — "Design a parking lot." What must happen before you touch the object model?

## A
Pin the scope out loud and in writing:

- **Core flows** (park, unpark, compute fee) — pick 2–3 and confirm they are what's graded.
- **Variants in scope** (vehicle types? spot types? multiple floors?).
- **Explicitly out**: payments, persistence, concurrency — say so and get a nod.

Scoping is itself graded; silently assuming requirements is the classic fail mode.
