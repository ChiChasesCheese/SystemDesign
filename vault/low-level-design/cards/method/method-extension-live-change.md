## Q
The interviewer doesn't ask how you'd extend it — they say "add weekend pricing now, you have 10 minutes." How is this scored differently from the verbal probe?

## A
The verbal probe scores your *story*; the live change scores whether the story was true. What's measured:

- **Number of existing files you open.** One new class plus one registration line is the target; opening the calculator and adding an `if` shows the seam was decorative.
- **Whether the earlier demo still passes** after the change — nobody's given credit for an extension that regresses the core flow.
- Whether you **re-run** before declaring done.

Announce the edit plan first ("new `WeekendFare` class, register it in the factory, no other file changes") so the diff can be checked against your claim.
