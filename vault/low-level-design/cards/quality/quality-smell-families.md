The five refactoring.guru smell families and their canonical members: {{c1::bloaters}} — long method, large class, long parameter list, primitive obsession, data clumps; {{c2::OO abusers}} — switch statements on type, refused bequest, temporary field, alternative classes with different interfaces; {{c3::change preventers}} — divergent change, shotgun surgery, parallel inheritance hierarchies; {{c4::dispensables}} — dead code, speculative generality, lazy class, duplicate code, data class; {{c5::couplers}} — feature envy, inappropriate intimacy, message chains, middle man.
