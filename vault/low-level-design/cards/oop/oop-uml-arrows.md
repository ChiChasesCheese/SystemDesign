UML class-diagram notation: inheritance is a solid line with a {{c1::hollow triangle}} at the parent; composition puts a {{c2::filled diamond}} at the whole; aggregation puts a {{c3::hollow diamond}} at the whole; dependency ("uses briefly") is a {{c4::dashed arrow}} toward the thing used.
