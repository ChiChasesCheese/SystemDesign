For a stock quoting bid $b$ and ask $a$: the **quoted spread** is {{c1::a − b}}, the **midpoint** is {{c2::(a + b) / 2}}, and the **half-spread** — the one-sided cost of trading relative to the midpoint, and the number usually quoted in bps — is {{c3::(a − b) / 2}}. The **effective spread**, which reflects what was actually paid rather than what was displayed, is {{c4::2 × |execution price − midpoint at order time|}}, and can be smaller than the quoted spread whenever an order receives price improvement. Empirically, US mega-liquid large caps trade with effective half-spreads around {{c5::1-5 bps}}, while illiquid small caps can run {{c6::20-100+ bps}} — since spread compensates for adverse selection and inventory risk, it widens with a name's information opacity and thinness rather than with its share price.

## zh
对于一只买价为 $b$、卖价为 $a$ 的股票：**报价价差（quoted spread）**是 {{c1::a − b}}，**中点（midpoint）**是 {{c2::(a + b) / 2}}，**半价差（half-spread）**——相对中点单边交易的成本，也是通常以 bps 报出的那个数字——是 {{c3::(a − b) / 2}}。**有效价差（effective spread）**反映的是实际付出的成本而不是显示出来的价差，等于 {{c4::2 × |成交价 − 下单时的中点价|}}，只要委托获得了价格改善，它就可能比报价价差更小。经验上，美股流动性极好的大盘股，有效半价差大约在 {{c5::1-5 个基点}}；而流动性差的小盘股则可能高达 {{c6::20-100+ 个基点}}——因为价差补偿的是逆向选择和库存风险，它会随一只股票的信息不透明度和流动性稀薄程度变宽，而不是随股价高低变宽。
