**Implementation shortfall** is the full gap between the paper P&L of executing instantly at zero cost and what actually happened, decomposed into named pieces so a single number can't hide where the cost came from. {{c1::Delay cost}} is the price move between the decision and the moment the order actually starts trading, applied to the full order quantity — it charges for the time between deciding and acting. {{c2::Execution cost}} is the gap between the price when the order started working and the actual average price paid on the shares that filled, applied only to the filled quantity — it bundles the algo's own {{c3::market impact}} with any adverse drift during the window that wasn't caused by the order itself. {{c4::Opportunity cost}} applies only to the portion of the order that never filled, measured as the move from the decision price to the price at the point the remainder was abandoned — it captures the cost of shares the fund wanted but never got, which a report that only looks at executed trades would silently omit. The benchmark all of these are measured against is the {{c5::arrival price (the price at the moment the decision to trade was made)}}, chosen specifically because, unlike VWAP, it is fixed before the order starts trading and so cannot be moved by the very execution being judged against it.

## zh
**实现盈亏差（implementation shortfall）**是"立刻、零成本成交"的账面盈亏与实际发生结果之间的全部差距，被拆分成若干个命名的组成部分，这样一个单一数字就没法掩盖成本到底来自哪里。{{c1::延迟成本（delay cost）}}是决策那一刻和订单真正开始交易那一刻之间的价格变动，作用在订单的全部数量上——它收取的是从决策到行动之间那段时间的代价。{{c2::执行成本（execution cost）}}是订单开始执行时的价格与实际成交股份平均支付价格之间的差距，只作用在已成交的数量上——它把算法自身的{{c3::市场冲击（market impact）}}和执行窗口内并非由订单本身造成的不利漂移打包在一起。{{c4::机会成本（opportunity cost）}}只作用于始终没有成交的那部分订单，衡量的是从决策价格到剩余部分被放弃那一刻价格之间的变动——它捕捉的是基金想要却始终没拿到的那些股份的成本，一份只看已成交交易的报告会悄无声息地把这部分完全遗漏。以上所有这些都是相对{{c5::到达价（arrival price，也就是做出交易决策那一刻的价格）}}来衡量的，之所以特意选择它作为基准，是因为与 VWAP 不同，它在订单开始交易之前就已经固定下来，所以无法被拿来评判它的那次执行本身反过来移动。
