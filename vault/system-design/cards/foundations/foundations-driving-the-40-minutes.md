## Q
Rough time structure for a 40-minute design interview you are expected to drive yourself?

## A
- **~5 min** requirements + scope agreement
- **~5 min** back-of-envelope estimates + API sketch
- **~10–15 min** high-level architecture end to end
- **~10–15 min** deep dive on 1–2 components (let the interviewer's interest or the hardest bottleneck pick which)
- **~5 min** wrap-up: bottlenecks, failure modes, what you'd do next

Signal comes from proposing the next step yourself, not waiting to be steered.
